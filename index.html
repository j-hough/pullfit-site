Put on your Meta Quest, pick up your resistance band and start training. PullFit tracks your hands while glowing targets guide each movement, helping you follow exercises without holding controllers.

Guided Workouts

Follow structured workouts and training plans designed around resistance-band strength training.

Mixed Reality

Fitness Tests

Test your repetitions and endurance, then track your performance over time with different resistance bands.
